Privacy

How the personal tools behind this site handle data.

The short version

This site and the personal applications it points to (including the OAuth client registered under this domain) are operated by Paul Gebheim for personal, non-commercial use. There is no business model here, and your data is not the product — because there is no product.

What the tools access

  • Google account data (Gmail, Calendar). The personal assistant tools access email and calendar data only to perform actions the account owner explicitly requests — reading a message, scheduling a reminder, searching mail.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on devices the account owner controls (personal computers and servers), encrypted at rest by the operating system, and are never transmitted anywhere except Google itself.

What never happens

  • No advertising, tracking pixels, or analytics on visitors.
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No retention of other people's data beyond what the account owner's own requests require.
