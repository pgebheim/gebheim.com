Summary

Hi, I’m Paul. I’ve spent the better part of 20 years building software that gets stuff done, makes things go, shows you what’s up, or keeps people connected — from real-time analytics at Justin.tv (before it became Twitch), to the first prediction market on Ethereum, to ZK at Polygon.

So I like software, but I love changing lives — whether that’s a beer social network (built one), a pay-it-forward fundraiser for charity: water (built that too), or a live-video app millions of people used (yep, that too). These days I run incubations at Sei Labs, where I’ve helped found two ventures and shipped an agent platform that runs whole companies.
